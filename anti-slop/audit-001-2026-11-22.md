# antislop audit 001 (2026-11-22)

antislop active: after (session override).

## Context

You asked how to keep KWeather's UI from looking like AI slop. The antislop skills are installed (core, ui, human, layoutmobile), and you picked **after** mode: audit what exists, you choose which numbered findings to fix. Your direction: the current design is good, make the backgrounds (the scene) and the app overall less generic, energy **Bold**, and one distinctive typeface for numbers.

Design Read: native Android weather app for people who check wind and alerts daily, in a code-drawn illustrated-scene style. Dials **ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2** (bold hero and color; fixed card order with varied weight; motion only where data drives it).

**Identity motif to build on:** the scene already moves with the real wind (rain slant, snow drift, cloud speed). That's the one thing no other weather app has. The fixes below make that the motif and remove scene parts that move or exist without data behind them.

**What already passes** (no action): copy is plain and specific with no em dashes, buzzwords or invented numbers; demo data says it's demo data; attribution is real; loading, error and no-permission states name the cause and give an action; motion respects Android's animator scale and the app toggle; icons are drawn in code and match their content; daily rows ≥44 dp, icon buttons 48 dp; alert colors have their own checked text colors.

## Findings

Priority follows antislop tiers: HIGH = Hard Gate, MEDIUM = Purpose-Gate, LOW = Quality Lock. ★ = your main ask (backgrounds).

| # | Pri | Rule | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | R-25 | Hero text on light skies fails contrast. Computed over the sky at the text's height: white temp/city is **2.2–2.7:1** on Fog and Snow at day/dawn/dusk (fails even the 3:1 large-text bar). The 17 sp condition line is ~3.1–3.5:1 on every daytime scene (needs 4.5). The H/L line (`#E6EEF7`, 15 sp) fails on 13 of 32 scenes, down to 1.9:1. The 25% text shadow doesn't count. | Add `heroInk` + `heroScrim` to `ScenePalette`: a soft darkening band behind the hero text, tinted from `skyTop` (not black), sized per palette so the worst spot reaches 4.5:1. Fog/snow days may get dark ink instead. H/L uses `heroInk` too. |
| 2 | HIGH | R-25 | Gust values in the 10-day list (12 sp, light-mode `gust #B0640A`) are 3.5–3.9:1 on light cards. | Darken light-mode `gust` until ≥4.5:1 on every light card (verified by test, #13). |
| 3 | HIGH | R-25 (non-text) | Sun yellow `#F3BD4E` on light cards is **1.3–1.5:1** (needs 3:1): sun-arc progress, sun glyph in the daily rows, storm bolt. | Add `sunInk` to the palette: the same yellow on dark cards, a deeper amber on light cards (≥3:1). Used by `ConditionIcon`, `SunMoonCard`. |
| 4 | HIGH | R-03 | Hero is a fixed 480 dp. On a short phone or in landscape it fills the screen and the first card starts off-screen. | Size the hero from window height (about 58%, clamped 360–520 dp) in `HomeScreen.kt`; the scrim math already reads `heroPx`. |
| 5 ★ | MEDIUM | R-13, R-19 | Sun has a radial glow plus an endless 6% pulse plus rays that spin forever. It's the only big motion in clear weather and it says nothing about the weather. | Static sun disc, keep one soft halo, drop the spinning rays and the pulse. |
| 6 ★ | MEDIUM | R-19 | Shooting star every exactly 11 s, lightning every exactly 7 s: visible loops after a few seconds. | Seeded irregular intervals: shooting star rare (roughly every 45–120 s), lightning jittered 4–12 s, more often when `heavy`. |
| 7 ★ | MEDIUM | R-22, R-07, R-20 | The same three green clip-art hills sit under every scene, every place. It's the only scene layer with no data behind it and the strongest "flat illustration template" tell. | Replace `drawHills` with a **wind-driven grass horizon**: a low ground band whose top edge carries ~140 seeded blades (quadratic curves). Lean angle and direction come from wind speed/direction, sway amplitude from gusts; static lean when motion is off. Snow: white ground, short sparse stubs. Night: silhouettes. Fog: blades fade into the fog. The final background-colored lip stays so cards still blend in. Needs `windGusts` added to `SceneSpec` (default `0.0` so `DemoData` keeps compiling, then set in demo entries). |
| 8 ★ | MEDIUM | R-22 | Every cloud is the same silhouette (one oval + two circles) stamped 2–8 times. Overcast and rain look like scattered cartoon clouds, not a covered sky. | Seeded per-cloud puff layouts (3–6 circles, flat base, varied size). Overcast/Drizzle/Rain/Storm get a continuous cloud deck: 2–3 wavy bands across the top, drifting with the wind, plus a few lower scud clouds moving faster. |
| 9 | MEDIUM | R-06 | System Roboto everywhere; the hero is 104 sp Light with −4 tracking, the stock weather-app hero. | Bundle one numeric face for figures only (hero temp, wind values, tile values, chart labels) with tabular numbers; Roboto stays for words. **Recommend Archivo** (OFL, variable width + weight: wide and heavy for the hero, normal width for tables). Alternatives: Barlow Semi Condensed (road-sign plainness), Big Shoulders Display (loud condensed, hero only). Add `ui/theme/Type.kt` with `NumberFamily` and apply it in the cards. The font file needs a download from Google Fonts: I'll ask before fetching, or you drop the TTF into `app/src/main/res/font/`. |
| 10 | MEDIUM | R-14 | All eight sections are the same box: 28 dp radius, 18 dp padding, same tint, same small grey title. Nothing shows the wind panel is the point of the app. (That's what the "card rhythm" question meant.) | Same order as now, different weight. **Wind card** gets the scene sky as its background (a window into the scene) with white figures in the new face, contrast verified. **Detail tiles** become one container with thin dividers instead of six separate boxes. 24 h chart, sun & moon and 10-day stay standard cards. |
| 11 ★ | LOW | R-20, R-29 | Below the hero, the page is sky color mixed 86% toward white and cards 66%, so on light scenes the content is near-white pastel and the scene's color stops at the hills. | Lower the white mix (start around 0.72 page / 0.52 card) so the content keeps the scene's hue. Exact values set by the contrast test (#13) so `onCardVariant` stays ≥4.5:1. |
| 12 ★ | LOW | R-01 | Sky is a flat two-stop vertical gradient; dawn/dusk warmth isn't tied to where the sun is. | Three-stop sky with a lighter horizon band; at dawn/dusk a warm horizon glow on the sun's side (left at dawn, right at dusk, matching `drawSun`). |
| 13 | LOW | R-35 | Contrast is only checked by eye in code comments today, so a palette tweak can silently break it. | Add `ContrastTest` (JVM): for every `SkyType` × `TimeOfDay`, assert hero ink vs scrimmed sky, `onCard`/`onCardVariant`/`gust`/`rain` vs card (4.5:1) and `sunInk`/`accent` vs card (3:1).
